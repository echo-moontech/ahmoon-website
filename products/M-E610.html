M-E610 RTV-2 Silicone Rubber

Two-Component Platinum-Cure Mold Making Silicone

RTV-2 / Addition Cure

M-E610 is a two-component, platinum-catalyzed addition-cure RTV-2 silicone rubber engineered for precision mold making. It is flowable, cures at room temperature or with heat, and delivers FDA food-grade safety with low shrinkage (≤0.1%) and excellent flow leveling. Ideal for art crafts, candles, gypsum ornaments, architectural elements, prototyping and industrial molding.
