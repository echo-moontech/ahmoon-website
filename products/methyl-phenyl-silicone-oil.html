Methyl Phenyl Silicone Oil

Phenyl Methicone — Heat-Resistant Silicone Fluid

Grade: M-556

Methyl Phenyl Silicone Oil (M-556) is a premium heat-resistant silicone fluid with phenyl groups substituting a portion of methyl groups on the silicone backbone. This substitution dramatically improves thermal stability (up to 300°C), radiation resistance, and solubility in organic solvents compared to standard dimethyl silicone oils. Available in phenyl content ranging from 5% to 50%, M-556 is the preferred choice for high-temperature thermal transfer, cosmetic formulations, and precision lubrication.

Applications

High-performance heat transfer medium for oil heaters, thermal oil baths, and industrial heating systems operating up to 300°C.

Conditioning agent in hair care products, skin creams, and lipsticks. Provides shine, smoothness, and improved rub-in properties.

Precision lubricant for plastic processing equipment, textile machine needles, and high-speed machinery components.

Mold release agent and processing aid for thermoplastics and high-performance rubber compounds.

Reaction medium and solvent for specialized chemical synthesis requiring high thermal stability.
