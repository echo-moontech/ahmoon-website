D-15 (ETAS) Ethyltriacetoxy-silane

Crosslinker for Acetoxy RTV-1 Silicone Sealant

RTV-1 / Acetoxy Cure

D-15 (Ethyltriacetoxy-silane, ETAS) is the essential crosslinker for acetoxy-cure (deacetic acid) RTV-1 silicone sealant. Upon exposure to atmospheric moisture it hydrolyzes and condenses, releasing acetic acid and forming a flexible, weather-resistant Si-O-Si network. It delivers fast skin-over, strong adhesion to glass, ceramics, metal and most building substrates, and is widely used in glass glazing, sanitary ware, general-purpose and weatherproofing sealants.

Applications

🪟 Glass & Window Glazing

Primary crosslinker for acetoxy glass glazing and curtain-wall sealants with fast skin-over and strong, durable glass adhesion.

🚿 Sanitary & Kitchen Sealant

Ideal for bathroom, kitchen and sink sealants thanks to water resistance and adhesion to ceramic, tile and stone.

🏗️ General-Purpose Construction

General-purpose acetoxy sealant for doors, windows, gaps and joints in construction and renovation.

🌧️ Weatherproofing & Aquarium

Weather-resistant sealing for aquariums, outdoor fixtures and HVAC where durable flexibility is required.

🧪 RTV-1 Formulation

Supplied to sealant manufacturers as a key RTV-1 crosslinker; compatible with PDMS gum and fillers.

🔧 Industrial Bonding

Used in industrial bonding and sealing of metal, plastic and electronic assemblies requiring acetoxy cure.
