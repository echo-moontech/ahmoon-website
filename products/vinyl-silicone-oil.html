Vinyl Silicone Oil

Vinyl-terminated Polydimethylsiloxane (Vi-PDMS)

CAS: 68083-18-1

Vinyl Silicone Oil (Vi-PDMS) is a vinyl-terminated polydimethylsiloxane with both ends capped by vinyl groups. Available in viscosities ranging from 100 to 100,000 mm²/s, it serves as the essential base polymer and crosslinker for addition cure (platinum-catalyzed) silicone rubber systems. It is also widely used in silicone sealants, waterproofing coatings, and polymer modification applications where thermal stability and flexibility are required.

Applications

🧴 Addition Cure Silicone Rubber

Base polymer for platinum-catalyzed addition cure silicone rubber (HTV & RTV-2). Provides excellent mechanical properties and transparency.

🔩 Silicone Sealants

Key raw material for structural adhesives and waterproofing sealants used in construction, automotive, and electronics assembly.

🏗️ Waterproofing Coatings

Elastic waterproof membranes for roof, bathroom, and infrastructure waterproofing with excellent UV and weather resistance.

🧪 Polymer Modification

Modifier for plastics, coatings, and adhesives to improve flexibility, thermal stability, and release properties.

⚡ LED Encapsulation

Optically clear encapsulation resins for LED modules, displays, and photovoltaic junction boxes.

🏭 Mold Making

Precision mold manufacturing for prototyping, candle making, jewelry, and art casting applications.
