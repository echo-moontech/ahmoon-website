Dimethyl Silicone Oil

Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS)

CAS: 63148-62-9

Dimethyl silicone oil (PDMS) is a versatile, inert silicone fluid with excellent thermal stability, low surface tension, and water repellency. Available in multiple viscosities from 100cst to 10000cst, it serves diverse applications across industrial, cosmetic, and medical industries. Our PDMS meets FDA and REACH standards for safe use in sensitive applications.
