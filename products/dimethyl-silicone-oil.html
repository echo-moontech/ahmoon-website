Dimethyl Silicone Oil

Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS)

CAS: 63148-62-9

Dimethyl silicone oil (PDMS) is a versatile, inert silicone fluid with excellent thermal stability, low surface tension, and water repellency. Available in multiple viscosities from 100cst to 10000cst, it serves diverse applications across industrial, cosmetic, and medical industries. Our PDMS meets FDA and REACH standards for safe use in sensitive applications.

Applications

🏭 Industrial Lubricants

High-temperature lubrication for bearings, gears, and machinery operating in extreme conditions.

💄 Cosmetics & Personal Care

Skin moisturizers, hair conditioners, and makeup bases. Provides silky feel and water resistance.

🏥 Medical & Pharmaceutical

Medical device lubrication, pharmaceutical processing, and implantable device coatings.

🍳 Food Processing

Food-grade anti-foam agents, release agents for baking and confectionery.

⚡ Electrical Insulation

Transformer oil, capacitor impregnation, and high-voltage cable insulation.

🛡️ Water Repellent

Textile waterproofing, building material protection, and automotive care products.
