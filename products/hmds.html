HMDSO Hexamethyldisiloxane

Silicone Intermediate / Blocking & Release Agent

CAS 107-46-0 / Siloxane Monomer (MM)

HMDSO (Hexamethyldisiloxane, also called MM) is a primary siloxane compound produced by dechlorination and condensation of trimethylchlorosilane. It is mainly used as a blocking (end-capping) agent, cleaning agent and release agent, and as a raw material for silazane synthesis. Widely applied in silicone rubber, medicine, gas chromatographic fixative, analytical reagents and water-repellent treatment.

Applications

🔒 Blocking (End-capping) Agent

Controls molecular weight and chain ends in silicone polymer synthesis by capping reactive silanol groups.

🧽 Release Agent

Provides a thin, non-reactive release layer for rubber and plastic molding; volatile with no residue.

🧪 Silazane Raw Material

Key precursor for the synthesis of polysilazane and other silicon-nitrogen compounds.

💧 Water Repellent

Hydrophobic treatment of powders, glass, textiles and inorganic surfaces for moisture protection.

🧴 Cleaning Agent

Effective cleaning and surface-conditioning agent in laboratory and industrial processes.

🔬 Analytical & GC

Used as a gas-chromatographic stationary phase and analytical reagent in research and QC.
