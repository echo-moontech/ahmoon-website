M-1401 Cyclopentasiloxane & Dimethicon Blend

Personal Care Silicone for Skin, Hair & Sun Care

D5 Blend / Personal Care Grade

M-1401 is a premium cyclopentasiloxane (D5) and dimethicon blend specifically formulated for personal care applications. It combines the light, volatile spreading of D5 with the film-forming smoothness of dimethicon to deliver a distinctive silky, non-greasy skin feel with excellent spreadability and emolliency. Ideal for skin care lotions, hair care serums, sun care formulas and color cosmetics.

Applications

🧴 Skin Care & Lotions

Light emollient for face creams, body lotions and moisturizers. Improves glide and absorption without a heavy residue.

💇 Hair Care & Serums

Adds silky shine and anti-frizz properties to hair serums, conditioners and leave-in treatments without weighing hair down.

☀️ Sun Care Formulas

Improves the sensory profile of SPF products — light, non-sticky spreadability and a dry-finish feel preferred by consumers.

💄 Color Cosmetics

Used in foundations, lipsticks and BB creams as a pigment wetting agent and smooth texture additive for even application.

🧼 Antiperspirant & Deodorant

Volatile D5 carrier for active delivery in roll-on and spray antiperspirant formulations.

🧴 Make-up Remover

Gentle cleansing base for make-up removers and cleansing wipes with excellent oil-phase solubility.
