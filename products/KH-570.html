KH-570 Silane Coupling Agent

3-Methacryloxypropyltrimethoxysilane

CAS: 2530-85-0

KH-570 is a methacryloxy-functional silane coupling agent designed for UV-curable coatings, adhesives, and composite materials. Its methacrylate group enables copolymerization with acrylic and methacrylic resins, while the silane functionality bonds to inorganic substrates. Ideal for applications requiring fast curing, excellent weather resistance, and superior adhesion.
