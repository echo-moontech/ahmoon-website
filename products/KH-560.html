Frequently Asked Questions

What is KH-560 silane coupling agent used for?
KH-560 (3-Glycidoxypropyltrimethoxysilane, CAS 2530-83-8) is an epoxy-functional silane coupling agent used as an adhesion promoter in epoxy resins, polyurethane, acrylic coatings, sealants and adhesives. It chemically bonds organic polymers to inorganic surfaces such as glass, aluminum and steel, improving adhesion strength and moisture resistance.
What is the CAS number and specification of KH-560?
CAS number: 2530-83-8. Chemical formula C9H20O5Si, molecular weight 236.34. Appearance: colorless transparent liquid. Purity ≥98%. Density 1.065 g/cm3 at 25°C. Moon Technology supplies in 25 kg plastic drums.
What is the difference between KH-550 and KH-560?
KH-550 is amino-functional (NH2 group), best for glass fiber and epoxy/phenolic systems. KH-560 is epoxy-functional (epoxy ring), best for coatings, adhesives and sealants on metal and glass substrates, and for systems cured with amines or anhydrides. KH-560 usually gives better wet adhesion in epoxy coatings.

How do you use KH-560 in coatings and adhesives?
Typical dosage is 0.5-2% of total formulation. Pre-mix KH-560 into the resin phase before adding the hardener or curing agent. For waterborne systems, hydrolyze in water/ethanol at pH 4-5 for 15-30 minutes before adding. Full adhesion develops after complete cure.
