KH-560 Silane Coupling Agent

3-Glycidoxypropyltrimethoxysilane

CAS: 2530-83-8

KH-560 is an epoxy-functional silane coupling agent that provides superior adhesion in epoxy, polyurethane, and acrylic systems. Its unique epoxy group reacts with various resins while the silanol groups bond to inorganic surfaces. Ideal for electronics encapsulation, high-performance coatings, and composite materials requiring excellent chemical resistance.
