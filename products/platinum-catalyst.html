Platinum Catalyst (Karstedt) 8000ppm

Addition-Cure Silicone Crosslinker for LSR, RTV-2 & HTV

Karstedt / Pt(0) / Addition Cure

Karstedt's platinum(0) catalyst at 8000ppm (Pt basis) is the industry-standard catalyst for addition-cure (hydrosilylation) silicone systems including Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR), RTV-2 and High-Temperature Vulcanized (HTV) silicone. It delivers fast, reproducible crosslinking without generating any by-products, resulting in low compression set and excellent material properties. We also offer 5000ppm and custom concentrations. All shipments include cold-chain or heat-pack protection in cold weather to preserve catalyst activity.

Applications

🥛 Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR)

Precision injection-molded LSR baby care, medical and food-contact products. Fast cure cycle and consistent platinum dispersion are critical.

🔬 RTV-2 Silicone Molding

Two-part room-temperature vulcanizing silicone for mold making, prototyping and art casting where addition cure is preferred.

🌡️ High-Temperature Vulcanized (HTV) Silicone

Continuous or batch HTV processes for extruded profiles, molded parts and cable accessories requiring high physical strength.

🏥 Medical & FDA Silicone

Platinum-cure silicone for medical devices, catheter components and FDA food-contact articles where no peroxide residuals are permitted.

⚡ Potting & Encapsulation

Addition-cure silicone potting for electronic modules, LED displays and sensors requiring thermal cycling resistance.

🎨 Silicone Adhesives & Coatings

Addition-cure pressure-sensitive adhesives and protective coatings for aerospace, automotive and industrial surfaces.
