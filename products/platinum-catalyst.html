Platinum Catalyst

Karstedt Catalyst | Platinum Divinyltetramethyldisiloxane Complex

Pt Content: 1000-50000 ppm

Our platinum catalyst (Karstedt catalyst) is a highly active, stable platinum complex specifically designed for silicone rubber curing and hydrosilylation reactions. It provides rapid cure rates, excellent pot life control, and consistent performance in RTV-2 silicone rubber, LSR, and addition-cure silicone systems. Available in various platinum concentrations to match your process requirements.
