KH-550 Silane Coupling Agent

3-Aminopropyltriethoxysilane

CAS: 919-30-2

KH-550 is a versatile amino-functional silane coupling agent widely used as an adhesion promoter between organic polymers and inorganic substrates. It enhances bonding in fiberglass-reinforced plastics, coatings, adhesives, and sealants. With its high purity (≥98%) and excellent stability, KH-550 is the preferred choice for thermoplastic and thermosetting resin applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is KH-550 silane coupling agent used for?
KH-550 (3-Aminopropyltriethoxysilane, CAS 919-30-2) is an amino-functional silane coupling agent. Primary uses include glass fiber treatment, mineral filler coupling in plastics and rubber, adhesion promoter in epoxy and polyurethane coatings, silicone sealants, and surface modification of inorganic materials.
What is the CAS number of KH-550?
KH-550 CAS number is 919-30-2 (3-Aminopropyltriethoxysilane). Molecular weight 221.37 g/mol. Appearance: colorless to pale yellow transparent liquid. Purity ≥ 99%, refractive index 1.420 at 25°C.
What's the difference between KH-550 and KH-560?
KH-550 is amino-functional (NH2), ideal for epoxy, polyurethane and phenolic systems. KH-560 is epoxy-functional (CAS 2530-83-8), ideal for polyester, acrylic and epoxy coatings. Use KH-550 for amino compatibility; KH-560 for higher wet adhesion.

How do you use KH-550 in coatings formulation?
Typical dosage 0.5-2% of total formulation. Pre-dilute with ethanol or water (pH 4-5 with acetic acid), hydrolyze 15-30 min, add to resin under stirring. For glass/metal substrates, allow 24h cure at room temperature. Contact us for application-specific guidance.
