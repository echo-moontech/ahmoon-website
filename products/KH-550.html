KH-550 Silane Coupling Agent

3-Aminopropyltriethoxysilane

CAS: 919-30-2

KH-550 is a versatile amino-functional silane coupling agent widely used as an adhesion promoter between organic polymers and inorganic substrates. It enhances bonding in fiberglass-reinforced plastics, coatings, adhesives, and sealants. With its high purity (≥98%) and excellent stability, KH-550 is the preferred choice for thermoplastic and thermosetting resin applications.

Applications

🔧 Fiberglass Treatment

Enhances adhesion between fiberglass and resin matrices in FRP composites, improving mechanical strength and durability.

🎨 Coatings & Paints

Improves adhesion and corrosion resistance in industrial coatings, primers, and paint systems.

🔩 Adhesives & Sealants

Boosts bonding strength in epoxy, polyurethane, and acrylic adhesive formulations.

🏗️ Construction Materials

Surface treatment for concrete, stone, and ceramic tiles to improve water resistance and durability.

⚡ Electronics

Encapsulation and potting compounds for electronic components, providing moisture protection.

🚗 Automotive

Tire manufacturing and rubber compounding for improved filler-rubber interaction.
