Frequently Asked Questions

What is Anhui Moon Technology Co., Ltd.?
Anhui Moon Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of organosilicon products based in Bengbu, Anhui Province, China. founded in 2005, the company supplies silane coupling agents, silicone oils, platinum catalysts, RTV-2 silicone rubber, and silicone intermediates to B2B customers in Southeast Asia, South America, Europe, North America and the Middle East. REACH registered.
What products does Moon Technology manufacture?
Moon Technology manufactures 50+ organosilicon products across 4 main lines: (1) Silane coupling agents (KH-550, KH-560, KH-570 and other amino, epoxy, vinyl silanes); (2) Silicone oils (dimethyl, vinyl, hydroxy, methyl-phenyl, D5, D6); (3) Platinum catalysts (Karstedt 8000ppm and custom concentrations); (4) RTV-2 silicone rubber and HMDSO, OPSZ polysilazane, and other silicone intermediates. OEM and ODM services are available.
Is Moon Technology a manufacturer or a trading company?
Moon Technology is a direct manufacturer (factory), not a trading company. Our production site is in Bengbu City, Anhui Province, China. Factory-direct pricing, in-house QC, TDS/MSDS/COA provided with every shipment. Customer audits are welcome.
What is the MOQ and lead time of Moon Technology?
MOQ: 800 kg for commercial orders. Free 1 kg sample available. Lead time: samples ship within 24 hours; bulk orders 7-15 days depending on quantity. Worldwide shipping by air, sea or courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS).
Does Moon Technology offer free samples?
Yes. Free samples of 100-500g are available for quality evaluation; buyer only pays international shipping (DHL/FedEx account preferred, or freight collect). Sample TDS, MSDS and COA included. To request a sample, contact echo@ahmoontech.com with product name and quantity.
What certifications and quality standards does Moon Technology have?
REACH registered, and supplies products meeting FDA 21 CFR 175.300/173.340/177.2600 for food-contact applications where applicable. Factory audits are welcome. All shipments include batch COA, TDS and MSDS.
